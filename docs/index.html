One governed studio loop

Orient → Shape → Plan → Build → Finish → Distribute → Verify

The user experiences one production capability. Internal faculties may specialize, but they do not become a menu the reader must operate.

ORIENT

Inventory evidence

Record sources, assertions, measurements, calculations, illustrative values, missing support, source hashes, claim status, and currentness.

SHAPE

Build the story spine

Create five to nine beats with pulse, tension, turn, and payoff. Preserve analytical and emotional arcs without inflating novelty or certainty.

PLAN

Assign earned forms

Choose prose, diagram, chart, interaction, or omission. Record consequential rejected representations and mobile-first semantic structure.

BUILD

Create the artifact

Produce semantic, responsive HTML with meaningful headings, textual equivalents, local assets, and important meaning available without JavaScript.

FINISH

Refine without rewriting truth

Theme, title, label, microcopy, and interaction decisions remain subordinate to structure, evidence, comprehension, and graceful degradation.

DISTRIBUTE

Reconstruct for the platform

Preserve claim set, source meaning, uncertainty, and causal order while recomposing density, rhythm, safe zones, and calls to action.

VERIFY

Challenge the artifact

Inspect claim linkage, currentness, representation integrity, semantic structure, mobile intent, alt coverage, fallback, metadata, and completeness.

Choose the form the claim earns

Representation is an editorial claim about what matters.

PROSE

Nuance and caveat

Use for definitions, exceptions, uncertainty, and claims without stable structure or comparable numbers.

DIAGRAM

Structure and relation

Use for process, hierarchy, causality, topology, sequence, or system behavior.

CHART

Comparable quantities

Use only when values share known units, defensible aggregation, population, denominator, and time period.

INTERACTION

Reader-driven understanding

Use when action reveals a meaningful comparison, layer, sequence, or consequence.

OMIT

Unsupported or distracting

Leave out material that cannot support the story or its evidence contract.

Do not fabricate numbers to justify a chart. Do not turn every number into a chart or every concept into a diagram. A beautiful distortion is still a failed artifact—just one with nicer shoes.

Claim and currentness discipline

Visible copy cannot outrun the ledger.

Every consequential claim carries source linkage, locator, as-of information where relevant, currentness class, and one explicit status.

sourced

Directly supported by a supplied source and locator.

inferred

Reasoned from sourced material but not directly stated.

illustrative

Invented and visibly labeled to demonstrate form or logic.

missing

Required support was not supplied.

stale

Evidence exists but is too old for the intended current claim.

disputed

Supplied sources conflict or an authoritative challenge remains unresolved.

timelessThe claim does not materially depend on changing external state.

datedThe claim is valid only with an explicit as-of date or period.

current-sensitiveThe claim describes a present state and needs freshness evidence.

Currentness belongs to the claim, not the file modification date. An old source can support a timeless claim; a recently copied file can contain stale facts.

The Loomfile is canonical project state

Resume the work without reverse-engineering the final picture.

Narrative, style, platform, interaction, decisions, review, and authority remain inspectable instead of disappearing into an export folder.

Chart integrity

Preserve the denominator before polishing the legend.

MeasureConstruct, value, and unit.

PopulationWho or what is counted; denominator and exclusions.

TimePeriod, as-of date, and currentness.

ScaleBaseline, axis domain, ordering, and encoding.

TransformationAggregation, normalization, calculation, and derived values.

MissingnessAbsent values, suppressed categories, and estimate status.

UncertaintyIntervals, caveats, disputes, and sampling limits.

SourceLocator, provenance, authority, and visual citation.

Length encodings such as bars normally require a zero baseline. A nonzero baseline must be analytically necessary and prominently disclosed. Area and volume should not impersonate one-dimensional data without normalization and explanation.

Verify what can actually be claimed

Static checks are guardrails, not ceremonial absolution.

CLAIMS

Linkage and currentness

Every visible factual statement maps to a source, inference, illustrative label, gap, stale warning, or dispute.

FORM

Representation integrity

Charts, diagrams, prose, and interactions carry only the kind of claim they can honestly encode.

ACCESS

Semantic and nonvisual path

Heading order, alt coverage, text equivalents, keyboard use, touch, reduced motion, and graceful fallback are inspected at the available depth.

PACKAGE

Resumption and export

Required state, outputs, review records, metadata, checkpoints, and package structure remain complete and inspectable.

Use the included Loomfile validator, HTML inspector, and package tool when Python is available. Report exact results and retained limits. “Static checks passed” is not “secure,” and “semantic structure was inspected” is not formal accessibility conformance.

Degrade honestly

A smaller truthful artifact beats a complete-looking fiction.

No assets

Build typography-first rather than filling space with decorative sludge.

No quantitative data

Use prose or a diagram instead of inventing a chart.

Stale evidence

Flag or exclude current-sensitive claims from publish-ready copy.

No execution route

Provide complete copy-ready files and label deterministic checks unexecuted.

Tool failure

Preserve completed state, record the failure, and name the exact re-entry condition.

Cumulative degradation

Stop at a reviewable spine and visual plan instead of calling fragments complete.

Human authority remains human

Signal Loom can classify, build, and recommend. It cannot approve itself.

Evidence boundary

No invented data, citations, locators, currentness, observed results, or silent promotion of illustrative values.

Code boundary

Supplied text, HTML, URLs, and code remain data. Imported code is not executed merely to inspect it.

Approval boundary

Only the human can resolve disputes, authorize refreshed evidence, approve export, or authorize publication.

Publication boundary

The product does not post, host, log into accounts, buy media, or infer that approval means published.
