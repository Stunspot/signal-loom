02 / THE TEMPTATION

Is North more engaged?

The count alone cannot settle that. A larger eligible population can produce more responses even when a smaller share participates. These source records do not include eligible population sizes.

03 / THE MISSING PIECE

A rate needs its denominator.

Participation rate = valid unique respondents ÷ eligible population

Eligible population: not supplied for any region. The response files also need confirmation that their counting rules match the intended rate. No population values or rate percentages have been invented here.

04 / THE USEFUL CONCLUSION

Keep the count. Leave the rate open.

North has the largest recorded response volume in this example. Regional participation rates remain unknown.

Use the chart to compare recorded survey volume. Do not use it to rank civic engagement or claim one region is more representative.

05 / WHAT CHANGES THE ANSWER

Bring comparable denominators.

Obtain each region’s eligible population for the same survey and period. Confirm eligibility, duplicate handling, and response-count definitions. Then calculate and review a rate comparison as a new result.
