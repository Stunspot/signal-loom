# Fictional survey material

These invented values teach the distinction between counts and rates. All records describe one fictional June 2026 survey window. They are not evidence about an actual population.

Missing fields: no eligible population sizes are supplied, and final unique-respondent/eligibility rules are not established.

Interpretation rule: compare recorded counts directly, but calculate participation rates only after compatible eligible-population denominators and valid unique respondent counts are supplied for the same period.
